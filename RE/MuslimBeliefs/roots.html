The 5 Roots of Shi'ism

Oneness of God He is the one that created everything. Nothing is like God — He is the ultimate power. More on it here.

Prophets These are men God sent to guide us. They teach us how to live good that to get into heaven and please God. More on it here.

Day of Judgement When the truth of Islam is to be revealed. More on it here.

Justice God created the world in a fair and just manner. More on it here.

Imamah There are 12 descendents of the prophets known as 'Imams' who hold special powers from God.

The Hidden Imam (Only some Shi'as) He is the Mahdi that is hiding, waiting to guide us. Some believe the 12 imams have a connection with him.

The Importance These 5 roots are important because: They are the foundation of Shi'ism. Without them, you cannot call yourself a (Shi'a) Muslim. They show a Shi'a what they must believe in to be a Muslim, They are sacred from coming from the Qur'an and Twelve Imams, And Without them, you will not be able to understand how to be a Muslim nor go to heaven.
