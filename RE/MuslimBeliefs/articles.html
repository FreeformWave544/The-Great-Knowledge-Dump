The 6 Articles of Faith

Belief In Allah Muslims believe that He (Allah) is the creator of the universe, and as such, is omnipotent and omnibenevolent. They also believe in Allah's oneness (Tawhid). There is only one god, so He has no partners, helpers, and above all else, no equals. That means no "Son" of God or similar. Since He is the only God, He must be the creator of everything, and in control of everything. A major characteristic of Allah is His benevolence, as all surah begin with: In the name of Allah, Most gracious, Most merciful. This means that, not only is Allah good, but He is also kind and loving to His creations. When He created humans, He did not leave us alone, and rather sent prophets to show us how to live our lives in order to get into heaven. Allah will forgive all sins repented for. Not only is God transcendent, but He also is immanence, meaning he is here, with us. Always. He can be contacting by humans through thought and prayer, stating in the Quran that: I am nearer to man than his jugular vein. Allah is also transcendent. This refers to the aspects of God's nature and power which is beyond human imagination. We cannot even begin to understand what God is nor how He works.

Belief In Prophets To be a Muslim, one must believe in all of the prophets, from Adam to Mohammed, with Mohammed being the last prohpet. The have come to guide humans, and are: 1. Adam 2. Idris 3. Nuh 4. Hud 5. Saleh 6. Ibrahim 7. Lut 8. Ismail 9. Ishaq 10. Yaqub 11. Yusuf 12. Shu'aib 13. Ayyub 14. Dhul-Kifl 15. Musa 16. Harun 17. Dawud 18. Sulayman 19. Ilyas 20. Al-Yasa 21. Yunus 22. Zakariya 23. Yahya 24. Isa 25. Muhammad

Belief In Angels This is how God communicated with humans. Jabril (Gabriel) was sent to give the message of Islam to Mohammed. And he sent Jabril to tell Mary that she was to have a baby. Jesus.

Belief In Holy Books The Qur'an, Hadith, Sunnah, and Shari'ah Law instruct Muslims on how to live their lives in order to get into heaven. As well as the Injil (New Testament), the Tawrat (Torah), which are believed to have been changed over time. So, the Quran is the final word of Allah to humanity.

Belief In The Day Of Judgement This is when the truth of Islam is revealed to humanity. It is when all people will be judged for how they behaved in their lives. Either the wait for this day is swift and painless, if you were a true believer, or Long and painful, if you were not a Muslim and did not believe in the faith.

Belief In The Afterlife This is the incentive Muslims have to follow the strict rules. Life is short, and not meant to be forever. Eternal life is present in the afterlife — heaven and hell. Only God can judge who will enter heaven or hell and, despite the rules, Muslims must never judge others. God is the ultimate judge.
