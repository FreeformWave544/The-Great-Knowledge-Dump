The Nature of God

Oneness of God Muslims must believe that there is only one God. And as such, Skirk (associating partners with God) is one of the worst sins. They also believe in Allah's:
  • omnipotence (power),
  • beneficence (mercy),
  • Adalat (justice),
  • transcendence (beyond imagination),
  • and immanence (always close).
Allah is also the creator: The one that made the universe and everything in it, The greatest: the perfection of greatness and power at every moment and in every incident, The merciful: the one who will have mercy when you die, And The Absolute Ruler: the one who controls everything and it is all part of His plan. And these are just some of the 99 names of God that depict His nature, found in the Qur'an. Most Muslims, when praying, will say 3 of the names 33 times, to make up a total of 99. Importantly, these 99 names help Muslims gain some understanding of the 'unknowable' nature of Allah, And with this understanding can show Allah more respect. This allows Muslims to get closer to Allah, and have a meaningful relationship with Him. This lets Muslims become accepted by the Ummah (Muslim community), bringing all Muslims closer together. And since these names are all contained within the Qur'an, Muslims believe Allah wants them to know these characteristics.
