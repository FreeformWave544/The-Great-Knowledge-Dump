Keywords

Muslim Beliefs
Word Meaning
Hadith The sayings of the prophet Mohammed.
Iman Faith
Sunnah Something the prophet did.
Shari'ah Law The Holy law of Islam that covers all aspects of life.
Tawhid Belief in the oneness of God (Monotheism).
Risalah Belief in Allah's angels, prophets and holy books. Literally meaning 'a message'.
Akirah The day of judgement.
Malaika Angels.
Adalat God's justice.
Nubuwwah Prophets of God.
Caliph Leader of the Islamic community.
Bismillah In the name of God (done before reading a verse of the Qur'an).
Surah A verse from the Quran
Sawm Fasting during Ramadan.
Hajj Pilgrimage.
Transcendence Beyond human experience.
Immanence God as being close to humans.
Salah Ritual prayer (5/day).
Khalifah Allah's stewards (the belief that we are all responsible for Earth — it's a gift from God).
Jibril The Archangel Gabriel
Shaytan The Devil (Satan)
Decree An official order from a high authority (e.g. God).
