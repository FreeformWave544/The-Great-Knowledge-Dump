Fossils and Evidence of Evolution

Evolutionary Trees These are models showing the evolutionary history and relationships among various species or organisms. The main sources of information for these models are collected are fossil records, Although scientists also compare the age, structure, and shape of fossils to work out the order in which the species evolved. We can also compare DNA sequences and other molecules, (e.g. proteins), for more accurate evolutionary trees of living organisms.
