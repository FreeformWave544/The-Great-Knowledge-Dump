New Systems of Classification

Three-Domain System of Classification The Three-Domain System of classification, proposed by Carl Woese (and two other chaps), was made by analysing genetic information. From this they found out that some species were not as closely related as they looked. In this system, all life is divided into 3 large groups. What are these groups called? Domains. These domains are: The Archaea (single-celled prokaryotic organisms), The Bacteria (single-celled prokaryotic organisms), And... The Eukaryota (a domain made up of animals, plants, fungi, and protists). Archaea and Bacteria similar in shape and size, but very different biochemically. Archaea are more closely related to eukaryotes than they are to Bacteria. The first Archaea to be discovered were extremophiles: Organisms adapted to live in very extreme environments e.g. salt lakes, oil wells, or hot springs.
