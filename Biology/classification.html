Classification

Linnaean System of Classification Kingdom, Kingdoms Phylum, Phyla Class, Classes Order, Orders Family, Families Genus, Genera Species. Keep Ponds Clean Or Frogs Get Sick Key words: Classify: Arrange into categories. Convention: The way in which something is usually done. Structure: The way the parts of something ar arranged. Taxonomy: The classification and naming of organisms.

Binomial System This system uses the genus and species as the name (e.g. Carcinus maenas) rather than common names (e.g. magpie). Breaking up the word, you get: Bi: Two (Think of the word bisexual: Attraction to two (or more) genders.) nomial: parts This system is also known (informally) as the scientific name or Latin name. And one of the many uses of this system can be to study evolution!
